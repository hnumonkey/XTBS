## Welcome

Welcome to the **Xiao Lab** in the School of Electrical, Electronic and Mechanical Engineering, a part of Center for Device Thermography and Reliability
(CDTR) at the **University of Bristol**.

Our research focuses on **ultra-wide-bandgap semiconductor materials and devices** for next-generation **power electronics**, **space electronics**, and **radiation-hardened technologies**. We combine advanced thin-film epitaxy, materials characterisation and device engineering to develop semiconductor platforms capable of operating under extreme electrical, thermal and radiation environments.

---

## Research Areas

Our current research includes:

- Gallium oxide (Ga₂O₃) ultra-wide-bandgap semiconductors
- Rutile germanium dioxide (r-GeO₂)
- Diamond electronics
- Mist-CVD epitaxial growth
- Thin-film processing and heteroepitaxy
- Schottky and p–n junction devices
- High-voltage and high-temperature power electronics
- Radiation-tolerant electronic devices for space applications

---

## Principal Investigator

**Dr Xiao Tang**

Assistant Professor

School of Electrical, Electronic and Mechanical Engineering

University of Bristol

Dr Tang's research aims to establish new ultra-wide-bandgap semiconductor platforms for future power electronic systems. His group develops novel epitaxial growth technologies together with advanced electrical and structural characterisation to enable next-generation semiconductor devices based on **Ga₂O₃**, **rutile GeO₂** and **diamond**.

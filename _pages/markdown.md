We are always looking for highly motivated students who are interested in semiconductor materials, epitaxial growth, power electronics and electronic devices.

## PhD Opportunities

Prospective PhD students with a strong background in one or more of the following areas are encouraged to contact us:

- Physics
- Electrical and Electronic Engineering
- Materials Science
- Semiconductor Devices
- Artificial Intelligence for Materials Research

If you are interested in joining Xiao Lab as a PhD student, please send the following documents:

- Curriculum Vitae (CV)
- Academic transcripts
- A brief statement of research interests
- Publications (if available)

📧 **Email:** xiao.tang@bristol.ac.uk

Suitable candidates will be advised on available funding opportunities and the application process at the University of Bristol.

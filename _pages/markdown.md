# Join Xiao Lab

We are always looking for highly motivated students who are interested in semiconductor materials, epitaxial growth, power electronics, and electronic devices.

## PhD Opportunities

Prospective PhD students with a strong background in one or more of the following areas are encouraged to get in touch:

- Semiconductor physics
- Electronic materials
- Materials science
- Electrical and electronic engineering
- Physics
- Artificial intelligence for materials research
- Thin-film growth and characterisation

If you are interested in joining the group, please send the following documents by email:

- Curriculum Vitae (CV)
- Academic transcripts
- A brief statement of research interests
- Any relevant publications (if available)

📧 **Email:** xiao.tang@bristol.ac.uk

We welcome enquiries from outstanding students worldwide. Suitable candidates will be advised on available funding opportunities and the application process at the University of Bristol.
